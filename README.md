# Graystrike

Role & Objective Design a high-converting, ultra-clean SaaS landing page for GrayStrike. The design must prioritize user understanding, immediately conveying value without technical jargon.

### Visual Identity - **Color Palette:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) background with vibrant Sky Blue (`#0284C7`) accents and deep Navy typography for readability. - **Aesthetics:** Minimalist, modern, and trustworthy.

Use ample whitespace, clean borders, and subtle shadows. --- ## Page Structure & Content ### 1.

Navigation Bar - **Logo:** "GrayStrike" text accompanied by a modern, minimalist shield icon. - **Links:** Platform, The Problem, How It Works, Trust & Compliance.

- **CTA Button:** Solid Sky Blue button labeled "Start Securing". ### 2.

Hero Section - **Headline:** Autonomous Cyber Security. Automated Code Repair.

Zero Effort. - **Sub-headline:** Stop worrying about security gaps.

GrayStrike automatically finds vulnerabilities in your digital systems and fixes them instantly with zero false positives. - **Interactive Input:** A prominent input field reading "[ Enter your website or app URL...

]" next to a button labeled "Scan Free". - **Visuals:** A clean, realistic preview image demonstrating the automated audit process.

### 3. The Problem & Solution Section - **The Problem:** Three clearly articulated cards highlighting common business pain points: Endless False Alarms, Slow Patching Cycles, and Human Error.

- **The Solution:** Three corresponding cards showcasing GrayStrike's benefits: Zero False Positives, Instant Auto-Repair, and 24/7 Protection. ### 4.

How It Works (3-Step Flow) Clear, visual step cards with intuitive icons: 1. **Connect Asset:** Enter your website or repository URL.

2. **Autonomous Scan:** Intelligent analysis runs quietly in the background.

3. **Instant Fix:** Review verified fixes and apply them with one click.

### 5. Enterprise & Compliance Integration A dedicated section detailing how GrayStrike simplifies standards like NIS 2 and ISO 42001, turning security compliance into a competitive advantage.

### 6. Social Proof & Trust Testimonials from industry leaders highlighting reduced remediation times, accompanied by security badges and metrics.

### 7. Footer Clean closing layout with quick links, copyright information, and a final "Secure Your Business" call to action.
